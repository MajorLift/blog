title: "A Scale With No Negative Region"
date: 2026-08-03 00:00:00 +0000
---

*Six instruments an agent built in one day to measure the effect of human corrections on its own work. Every one of them could record that a correction happened, and none could record that a correction made things worse, because the construct was never written down and the cheapest available signal filled the slot instead.*

Left to itself, a measure comes out able to register that something happened and unable to register that it went wrong. Six instruments in one day carried that defect, and the pattern behind it is a matter of what a scale can express rather than of how carefully it is applied. The agent built every one. I directed the work and supplied the corrections. Seven times what I supplied was the dimension a measure was missing. That asymmetry is not a gap in what the measure covers. It is a censoring of what the measure can say. An instrument built this way does not fail to find harm. It lacks anywhere to put the harm it finds. The pattern is not a matter of care. Arithmetic held throughout the day, and the samples were large enough. What a measure is able to express is inherited from the moment the measure is built, rather than from how carefully the measure is then run.

Inheritance of that kind is invisible while it happens, since nothing announces the moment a measure acquires its range. Impact was operationalized four times before it was defined once. Nothing in the day's notes said what the impact of a correction was supposed to mean. Each operationalization reached for whatever the parsing pipeline had already produced. First the number of turns released after a correction, then the minutes elapsed before the next one, then whether a commit landed within an hour, then five regex categories over the resulting diffs. Each of the four is a real quantity, each was cheap because the data was already in memory, and each silently became the definition of impact for as long as it survived. An undefined construct does not sit empty and wait. The slot gets filled by whatever is nearest to hand. The filling leaves no trace, because a proxy adopted by default looks identical to a proxy adopted on purpose.

A default that fills a slot quietly is one problem. A default that starts generating findings is another, and the commit-window measure produced the day's most attractive claim: that corrections which reframe a task are penalized by a proxy rewarding shipping speed, since reframing sends work backwards while a commit inside the hour is what the proxy counts. The claim was reported as confirmed on the strength of 86 cases that agreed with it, none of which had been selected for their capacity to disagree. A direct test refuted the claim in one query. The reframing family commits within an hour 37 percent of the time against 36 percent for the artifact-directed family, and the commit rate rises with the reach of a correction rather than falling, so the corrections that touch the most downstream work are followed by commits more often, not less. The instrument had generated a story about its own bias. A story about bias is more interesting than a null. That is why it survived a reading of 86 cases and died to the first reading that admitted a disagreeing case.

Admitting a disagreeing case is what a control does by construction, which is why controls kill instruments that readings cannot. The five-category diff reader died to one that shipped in the same run as the measure. Timestamps were randomized and the categories re-scored against windows drawn at random. The five families came back at proportions of 30.0 to 33.7 percent against 29.3 percent for the random control, which puts every family inside the noise. A second check finished the reader off, since each of the five categories correlated with the raw volume of changes in its window at r between 0.80 and 0.99, so the five categories were one variable wearing five labels. The control cost minutes to write, because the randomization ran over data already loaded. It retired an instrument that had taken most of an afternoon.

What made that instrument reachable was where its defect sat. The diff reader was wrong in its output. Output is the one thing every check already reads. The severity scale hid its defect somewhere else. That makes it the clearest instance of the pattern, because nothing in the scale was wrong at the level of execution. Its four levels ran negligible, contained, material, decisive, and all four are non-negative. Non-negative here is a fact about range rather than about tone, since the scale's least value is the absence of effect, so the worst thing the scale can say about an intervention is that the intervention did nothing at all. An intervention that made the work worse had no level to land on, so the scale could record that a correction mattered while staying silent on which direction the correction moved the work. The reported figure, 41 percent material or worse, means that 41 percent of the corrections mattered. It was being read as 41 percent helped. Those two readings differ by the entire question the day set out to answer.

A gap that wide should be loud. It stays silent because a unipolar scale reads as rigorous. That appearance is what makes the defect durable rather than embarrassing. The levels are ordered, raters can be trained on them, agreement computes on them, and the resulting alpha is a correct number about a scale that cannot represent harm. No internal quality check fires, because every check available inside an instrument is a check the instrument was built to pass. Inter-rater agreement asks whether raters apply the levels consistently rather than whether the levels span the outcomes. A distribution over four categories is well-formed whether or not a fifth belonged in the set. Coverage statistics report the fraction of items scored rather than the fraction of possible results the scoring could express. Each of those checks reads the instrument's output. The defect lives in the instrument's range, where nothing was looking.

Nothing was looking there because the range is not something an instrument reports on. Widening it is the repair. The signed pass built to widen it needed a null of its own. When a scale with a negative region was finally built, the positive category failed a floor test, since task assignments and procedural turns, inputs that corrected nothing and could not have improved anything, were graded improved by 17 to 27 percent of raters. Adding the negative region fixed what the scale was able to represent, but the raters still had no anchor at zero, so the positive category absorbed null items at a rate high enough to move any aggregate built from those scores. A scale can be well-shaped and still uncalibrated. The floor test is the cheaper of the two diagnoses, because null items can be manufactured from material already in the corpus.

Two failures, then, not one: a range that cannot express harm, and a zero the raters never anchored. Across the day three defects of that family recur. Their counts are the record. Scales with no negative region three times, classifiers with no null control six times, constructs instrumented before they were defined four times. Seven times an input from outside the instruments supplied a dimension that none of them carried. A count of interventions was met with a question about consequence, a frequency with a question about kind, a proxy with the construct it stood in for, a single bad instance with the pathology generating that instance, a kind with a degree, a degree with a sign, and a set of results with the question of what fraction of the source data those results covered. The checks the agent ran on its own work caught none of the seven. Those checks were not lax. Parsing was verified, aggregates were recomputed against their inputs, sample sizes were reported alongside every rate. Verification of that kind establishes that an instrument is doing what it was built to do, which is precisely the question that is not at issue when the instrument was built around the wrong quantity.

If verification cannot reach a wrongly chosen quantity, the remedy has to sit earlier, at the point where the quantity gets chosen. The remedies that sit there are structural. They are cheap enough that cost is not what keeps them out. Give every scale a negative region, including where harm is expected to be rare, since rarity is a finding the scale should be able to report and an absent level is a censoring rather than a prediction. Run the null control in the same run as the measure rather than deferring it, because a control deferred becomes a control competing against the next question, and the next question always arrives before the follow-up does. Write the construct down before choosing the instrument, in a sentence naming what would count as the thing failing to happen, since a definition that cannot describe its own negation is not yet a definition. None of the three is expensive relative to the instrument it guards. The diff reader was retired by a control that cost minutes.

Set against a price that low, what the day produced is a tally rather than a measurement. Three scales with no negative region, six classifiers with no null control, four constructs instrumented before definition, seven dimensions supplied from outside the instruments, and one surviving signed pass whose positive category still needs an anchor at zero. The measurement of impact was not made. What was established instead is the condition under which a measure of impact would be worth reading, and the first part of that condition is that the measure can come out negative.
